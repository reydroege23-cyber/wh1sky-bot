# Administrator Guide

Core moderation:

- `/ban`, `/unban`, `/kick`, `/mute`, `/unmute`
- `/tempban <user_id> <minutes> [reason]`
- `/tempmute <user_id> <minutes> [reason]`
- `/warn`, `/warns`, `/clear_warns`
- `/history`, `/modlogs`, `/modstats`, `/audit`
- `/purge`, `/purgeuser`, `/report`

Protection:

- `/guardian on|off`
- `/antispam on|off`
- `/antilink on|off`
- `/antiflood on|off`
- `/antiemoji on|off`
- `/antiraid on|off`
- `/scan`
- `/threatlevel`

Permanent re-ban:

- `/enough <user_id|reply> [reason]`
- `/enough list`
- `/enough remove <user_id>`

Alt detection reports are risk assessments only. They are not proof.
